# Code of Conduct

Parrot is a small, friendly project. Everyone is welcome here, whatever your
experience, background, identity or how good your Swift is.

## The short version

- Be kind. Assume good intent, and say so when you disagree.
- Critique code and ideas, never people.
- No harassment, insults, sexual content, or personal attacks. Not in
  issues, PRs, discussions, or anywhere else you're representing the project.
- Don't share anyone's private information, and that includes things people
  said on a recorded call.
- "I don't know" and "I got it wrong" are good answers. Beginners and
  AI-assisted contributors are welcome.

## If something's wrong

Email Uygar at uygar@openparrot.app. Reports are kept private. The
maintainer may edit or remove comments, close threads, or ban people who
don't follow this, and will explain why where it's safe to.

This code is based on the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/),
version 2.1, which covers the full details and applies here too.
