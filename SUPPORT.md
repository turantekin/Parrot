# Getting help with Parrot

1. **Read the guide.** [Parrot Help](https://turantekin.github.io/Parrot/help/)
   covers every feature and has a
   [Troubleshooting](https://turantekin.github.io/Parrot/help/troubleshooting.html)
   page for permissions, silent recordings and model downloads. It's also
   inside the app under **Help > Parrot Help**, offline.
2. **Ask a question** in
   [Discussions](https://github.com/turantekin/Parrot/discussions/categories/q-a).
3. **Report a bug** from inside Parrot: click the ladybug in the corner or
   **Help > Report a Bug**. It fills in your version and settings, and you
   check it before anything is posted. Or
   [open an issue](https://github.com/turantekin/Parrot/issues/new/choose).
4. **Security or privacy problem?** Please report it privately, see
   [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

Parrot is built by one person in their spare time, so replies usually take a
few days. Audio-privacy issues jump the queue.
