Connections: getting meetings out

A meeting's Share menu (top right) and Settings → Connections send your calls where your work happens. Everything here is off until you turn it on.

Markdown and your notes app

Export as Markdown writes the meeting as a note: the title, date and people at the top, then your notes, the summary, next steps as tick boxes, coaching, marked moments and the transcript. Pick a folder in Settings → Connections (an Obsidian vault works well) and every finished meeting can be saved there automatically. Re-saving a meeting updates the same note. When you rename a meeting that is already in the folder, its note is renamed too.

A note in the folder stays up to date when you name speakers, write or rewrite the report, or draft the follow-up email. A summary that is already written keeps the names it had, so name speakers before you write the report if you want them in it. If you edit a note yourself, Parrot leaves your text alone and only renames the file. Save to My Folder replaces the note with the latest version, your edits included. A note you delete or move stays that way.

Follow-up email

Draft Follow-up Email writes a short email with what was agreed and who does what, in the language of the call. It only lists promises somebody actually made on the call. The draft appears on the Report tab, with Copy and Open in Mail, already addressed to the calendar invitees. Nothing is sent until you send it. Turn on "Draft one after every call" to have it waiting.

Apple Reminders

Add Next Steps to Reminders puts the report's next steps and commitments into a Parrot list in Reminders. macOS asks once for permission.

Webhook (Slack, Notion, CRMs)

Paste a webhook address from Zapier, Make or n8n and Parrot posts each finished meeting there as JSON: title, date, people, summary, next steps, marked moments and notes, plus the transcript if you allow it. From there it can reach Slack, Notion, HubSpot or anything else those tools connect to. Only https addresses are accepted. Add a signing secret and every request carries an X-Parrot-Signature header (an HMAC-SHA256 of the body), so the other end can check it came from you. Send Test sends a sample. Redirects aren't followed, so a meeting only ever goes to the address you pasted. On-device-only meetings are never sent.

Claude and other AI apps

Let Claude, Codex or Cursor read your meetings, read-only: connect in one click, choose what they see, and ask things like "What did I promise last week?". It has its own page: Use your meetings in Claude. Settings → Connections has the main switch and Open Claude & AI Apps.
