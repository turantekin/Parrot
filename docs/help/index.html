Parrot Help

A call recorder that keeps everything on your Mac.

Parrot records both sides of a call, writes the transcript as you speak, and (if you want) has an AI copilot watching the conversation with you. Everything runs on your own machine. Nothing leaves it unless you plug in your own AI key, and even then only transcript text is sent, never audio.
