Ask Parrot

Parrot remembers every call it has finished. Ask it anything, like "What did I promise Acme?" or "What did Jeremy say about pricing in August?", and you get a short answer where every fact carries a small chip such as 12:34 · Acme renewal. Click the chip and that meeting opens at that exact line, ready to play.

Ask Parrot is new, so it's marked Beta. It's still learning and an answer can miss something, so click a time to check where it came from. A new chat has a Send feedback link: tell us what worked and what didn't.

Where to find it

Open Ask Parrot from the sidebar, press ⌘K anywhere, or click Ask on a meeting page to ask about that one call.

Chats you can come back to

Every question you ask starts or continues a saved chat, listed on the left. Click New chat to start fresh. Right-click any chat to Rename… or Delete it.

Follow-ups

Ask a follow-up like "and what did we offer them?" and Parrot keeps the thread, so you don't have to repeat who or what you're asking about.

Every answer cites its source

Click a time chip to open that meeting at that exact moment. In a chat about one meeting, chips show just the time. A chip that reads "deleted" means that meeting is gone.

Ask about one meeting by name

Name a meeting in your question, like "What did we decide in the Northwind call?", and Parrot reads that whole meeting to answer. This works for meetings you've given a name; ones still called "Meeting Sep 23…" are easier to reach with Ask on their meeting page.

Counts, lengths and who you met

Questions about your meetings themselves are answered on your Mac, exactly, with no AI:

  • "How many meetings did I have last month?"
  • "My 5 longest meetings"
  • "How much time did I spend in meetings this week?"
  • "Did I meet with Northwind?" or "How many meetings with Acme?": Parrot counts the meetings where the name appears in the title, the people, the report or what was said.

These answers say Counted on this Mac. Each meeting in the answer is a chip that opens it. A question about what was said, like "How many meetings were about pricing?", goes to the AI instead.

Broad questions

A question like "What did I promise this week?" covers many meetings at once. Words like today, yesterday, this week, last week, this month, last month, "the last 30 days" and a weekday ("on Monday" means the most recent Monday) narrow the search to that period.

Ask in Turkish or English

Ask in either language and the answer comes back in the language you asked in. Turkish time words work too: bugün, dün, bu hafta, geçen hafta, bu ay, geçen ay, son 30 gün, weekdays like pazartesi, and forms like dünkü or geçen haftaki. Counts work in Turkish as well, like "Geçen ay kaç toplantı yaptım?", "Ahmet'le kaç toplantı yaptım?" or "En uzun toplantılarım".

Which AI answers

Ask Parrot can use one of three AIs:

  • Claude (cloud): Anthropic's Claude, with your own API key from console.anthropic.com. You pay Anthropic per use, usually a fraction of a cent per question. A Claude.ai Pro or Max subscription can't be used here; it needs an API key.
  • Ollama (local): a free model that runs on your Mac. Nothing leaves it.
  • Custom server: any OpenAI-style server, like OpenAI, Groq or LM Studio, with its address (and a key if it needs one).

It uses your reports AI unless you pick another. The name in the top-right corner of the chat says which AI is writing the answer. Switch it there, or in Settings → Copilot → Model → Ask Parrot. Whichever you pick, the search itself runs on your Mac.

Which to pick: Claude gives the best answers, especially for questions across many meetings. A small local model through Ollama is good for questions about one meeting, and counts are exact either way, but it can mix up people or meetings when a question spans several.

If Ask's AI is Ollama and Ollama isn't open, or the model isn't downloaded yet, a message at the top of the chat says so, with Check Again or Download (size) to fix it.

No AI set up yet

A new chat shows Get written answers with two buttons: Use Claude (needs a key) and Use a free AI on this Mac. Without an AI, Parrot still works: it shows the closest moments from your meetings instead of a written answer.

What leaves your Mac

Search always runs on your Mac. Only the best passages and this chat's recent messages go to a cloud AI to write the answer; with a local model through Ollama, nothing leaves at all. Meetings recorded on-device only never leave your Mac, including in earlier answers that used them.

Stop

Stop cancels an answer that's still writing. Your question goes back in the box so you can edit or resend it.

"From your last call"

When your calendar is connected and a call starts with someone you've met before (same invitees, or the next meeting in a recurring series), the Copilot's Briefed card shows the open items from last time: next steps and promises from that meeting's report. The Copilot keeps them in mind and flags them when they come up. This is read straight from your own report, on your Mac, with no extra AI call.
